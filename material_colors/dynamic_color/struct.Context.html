pub fn in_light_standard<R>(self, f: impl FnOnce(Context<'_>) -> R) -> R

Runs f against a copy of the scheme in light mode at standard contrast. Palettes are identical, so the palette-peak memo is shared; role tones get a fresh memo. No copy when the scheme already is light at standard contrast.
